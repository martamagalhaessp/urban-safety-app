# User research

## Where the research stands

So far, the insights below come from **informal conversations with potential users and desk research**, organised into a first structured round of synthetic (simulated) interviews to sharpen the questions. **None of them is validated yet.** They are hypotheses, and the next step is to test them in real sessions.

I’m keeping them here with a confidence level so it’s clear what is known and what is assumed, and so each one can be updated as evidence comes in.

## Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Confidence | What it changes in the product |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The need peaks on **unfamiliar** routes; daily routes already feel “solved” | Medium | Target newcomers and people outside their usual area, not daily commuters |
| 2 | People judge how safe a street feels by **what they can see**: light, open places, people around | Medium | Explain routes in terms of visible conditions, not statistics |
| 3 | Small detours are acceptable; **beyond a certain extra time, people take a ride instead** | Medium | Cap the extra time of the safer route |
| 4 | People trust **people they know**, not anonymous strangers | Medium | Share the walk with trusted contacts; no public reviews in the first version |
| 5 | Emergency (SOS) apps get installed and forgotten; the **value moment is planning before leaving** | Medium | Focus on planning the route; leave SOS out |
| 6 | Streets can also feel unsafe when **empty during the day** (e.g. Sunday mornings) | Weak / anecdotal | Support any time of day; don’t reposition unless data shows it |
| 7 | Runners have a different need (finding lit loops, not getting from A to B) | Weak / anecdotal | Park as a separate idea for later |

## How I’ll validate them

- **5 real sessions** with potential users: a short interview plus a route comparison test (see [`experiments.md`](experiments.md))
- After each session, update the table: **supported / not supported / unclear**, with what was observed
- Hypotheses that aren’t supported lead to a decision: change the product, or drop the idea

## Research principles

- No names or personal details are published here
- Questions are about past behaviour (“the last time you walked somewhere unfamiliar at night…”), not opinions about the app
