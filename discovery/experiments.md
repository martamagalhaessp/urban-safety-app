# Experiments

Rule: every experiment has its success criteria written down **before** it runs.

## E1 · Do people agree with the “safer” route?

- **Hypothesis:** for a given trip, people walking at night would choose the route the app calls “safer”.
- **Method:** show 5 participants two routes for the same trip, without saying which one the app prefers, and ask which they would walk and why.
- **Success criteria:** at least 4 of 5 choose the app’s safer route.
- **Status:** ⏳ ready to run

## E2 · Does the app’s view of a street match people’s?

- **Hypothesis:** how the app rates a street matches how people who know it would rate it.
- **Method:** participants rate about 20 streets they know; compare with the app’s view.
- **Success criteria:** clear agreement between the two (rank correlation ≥ 0.6).
- **Status:** ⏳ ready to run

## E3 · Does the map match reality at night?

- **Hypothesis:** the street conditions the app relies on match what you see on the ground after dark.
- **Method:** walk 3 routes at night and check, segment by segment.
- **Success criteria:** at least 80% of segments match.
- **Status:** ⏳ planned

## E4 · Can people use the main flow without help?

- **Method:** usability test of the wireframes with 3–5 people. Task: “It’s 00:30. Plan your walk home and let a friend know when you arrive.” Observe without helping.
- **Success criteria:** most participants complete the task without help; every point where they hesitate is logged and fixed.
- **Status:** ⏳ after E1–E3
